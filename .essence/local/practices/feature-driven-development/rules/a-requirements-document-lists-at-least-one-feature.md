# A requirements document lists at least one feature

## Rules

- **R1.** A requirements document of an endeavor that applies this practice must list at least one feature record.
  - **Classification:** structural
  - **Enforced:** when an endeavor starts, and when its requirements document changes
  - **Violation response:** the agent plans at least one feature into the endeavor, and lists its record in the requirements document.

## Facts

- **feature record** — the vocabulary of this practice
- **requirements document**, **endeavor** — `language/vocabulary.md`

## Examples

- ❌ An endeavor starts with no feature — breaks R1. No feature then reaches a milestone, and the endeavor shows no progress.
- ✅ An endeavor starts. The agent plans by feature, and lists the record of each planned feature in the requirements document.

## Rationale

An endeavor that applies Feature-Driven Development has its requirements as features. A requirements document with no feature then states no requirement.

The progress of an endeavor is the progress of the features that its requirements document lists. With no feature, there is no progress to compute.
